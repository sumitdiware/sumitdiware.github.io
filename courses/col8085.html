Course Description

This course aims to provide an overview of modern AI computing systems from a hardware perspective. It covers the characteristics of contemporary AI workloads, current AI hardware systems that execute them, and their evolution towards emerging AI hardware systems.

The tentative course outline is as follows:

  1. Fundamentals of AI Software
  2. Introduction to Hardware System Design
  3. Conventional AI Hardware Systems
  4. Emerging AI Hardware Systems (near/in-memory, neuromorphic, etc.)
  5. Software Considerations for Emerging AI Hardware

As the course involves research-based content, we will also discuss effective strategies for reading and understanding research papers.
